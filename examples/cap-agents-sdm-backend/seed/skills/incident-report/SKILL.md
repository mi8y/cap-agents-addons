# Incident Report

Copy the [template](templates/incident-report.txt) into a new report, then fill it
from incident notes and relevant workspace documents.

- Read earlier reports for the same incident, if available. Build on their
  timeline and open actions, apply new facts, and leave earlier reports unchanged.
- Mark carried-forward items without updates "No update provided" and record
  source report paths in Sources and Gaps.
- Summarize what happened and the business impact; order events by timestamp
  and preserve the supplied timezone.
- Distinguish service recovery from permanent resolution, and suspected causes
  from confirmed root cause. Mark an open investigation clearly.
- Record corrective actions with supplied owners and due dates. Leave missing
  severity, causes, or other facts for confirmation.
